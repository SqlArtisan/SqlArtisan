perf: Read each part once in the duplicate-clause walk

The duplicate-clause walk still cost the benchmark query about a seventh of its
build: two FrozenDictionary probes and some thirty type tests per part. One
table keyed by exact type now gives a part's role and both its kinds in one
probe, and the exclusive pairs are read through a per-kind mask. The tables the
walk is built from are unchanged, so are its decisions and every message;
RequirementOnlyOnClause, which the type tests matched through its base, is
listed beside it, and DuplicateClauseWalkTests fails for a derived clause or an
ISelectItemsClause / IJoinedRelation implementer the table lacks.

The emitted SQL, the parameters and the allocated bytes are unchanged for every
statement.
